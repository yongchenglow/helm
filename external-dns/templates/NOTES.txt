external-dns has been deployed to namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Watch it reconcile the zones listed in `domainFilters`:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -f

A healthy startup logs "All records are already up to date" or the records it
created. Two failures are common:

  - "failed to get zones" usually means the Cloudflare API token in the
    external-dns-cloudflare secret is missing, expired, or lacks Zone:Read and
    DNS:Edit on the zones in domainFilters.
  - Records not appearing at all usually means no Ingress or Service carries a
    hostname inside domainFilters.

The chart runs with policy `upsert-only`, so deleting an Ingress leaves its DNS
record in Cloudflare. Remove those by hand, or switch the policy to `sync` in
values.yaml once you trust the TXT registry.
